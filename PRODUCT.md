# GiveLife studio

## Platform and stack
Existing responsive web app: Python HTTP API, vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Render hosting. Preserve this stack, all APIs, data and delivery rules.

## User and work
The owner manages a fundraising campaign, edits WhatsApp captions, sends group posts and individual donor appeals, and checks real sending activity. Often used under pressure, on Android with the right thumb and on a Windows desktop for extended sessions.

## Existing workflows
Assistant and full conversation history; group/DM drafts and inline editing; group sends and receipts; individual and pasted donor sends; donor search, importance, donation status and amount; wording bank; poster replacement; WhatsApp connection; settings, memory and corrections. These are verified in index.html, app.js, donors.js and connection.js. Separate case/agent management screens are not present.

## Requirements from the owner
Calm legible light/dark themes, minimal travel and steps, compact donor rows, centered readable replies, complete normal caption previews, real highlighted activity, accessible send/return controls, preserved drafts and history. A small amount of scrolling is acceptable for unusually long content. No real messages or transactions during testing.

## Tools
Impeccable and 16 Stitch skills are installed locally. Stitch MCP is disabled at the owner's instruction to ignore tools needing keys. gstack official installer did not complete; do not claim it is available. Existing UI UX Pro Max, Frontend Design and Web Design Guidelines remain available.
